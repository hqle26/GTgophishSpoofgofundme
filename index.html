Hello community,

The Spanish Speaking Organization (SSO) has been a cornerstone of our community for over a decade. Our mission is to empower, educate, and advocate for Spanish-speaking individuals and families in our region.

We provide essential services such as:

  • Free English as a Second Language (ESL) classes.
  • Legal and immigration resource workshops.
  • Community cultural events and youth mentorship.

Due to recent cuts in local funding, we are facing a significant budget shortfall. To keep our doors open and continue offering these vital services free of charge, we need your help.

Your donation will directly fund our educational programs and ensure that our community center remains a safe, welcoming hub for everyone. Whether you can give $5 or $500, every contribution makes a difference.

Thank you for standing with us. ¡Gracias por su apoyo!
