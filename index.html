Nuestra Misión

A Home Away from Home for Every Yellow Jacket

Founded at the Georgia Institute of Technology, the Spanish Speaking Organization (GT SSO) bridges cultures, fosters bilingual fluency, and provides an authentic familia for native speakers, heritage learners, and Spanish enthusiasts alike.

Whether you are an engineering student preparing for international co-ops, an international student missing mom's home cooking, or looking to connect with Atlanta's thriving Hispanic heritage, GT SSO creates the community and academic support where you can thrive.

🤝

Familia & Belonging

Weekly gatherings, mentorship pairings, and shared traditions that make a 16,000-student campus feel like home.

🎨

Cultural Celebration

Showcasing the vibrant richness of Latin America and Spain through dance, music, history, and food.

🚀

Academic & Career Leadership

Study-abroad scholarships, resume critiques, alumni networking, and professional conference sponsorships.

Official GT Student Org

Recognized student organization based at the John Lewis Student Center in Atlanta, Georgia.

Where Your Support Goes

As a student-led 501(c)(3) university organization, 100% of community contributions go directly back into empowering Georgia Tech students and sustaining our cultural traditions.

🎺

Cultural Performers

Funding live mariachi ensembles, salsa orchestras, and traditional folkloric dance instructors for campus galas.

🎓

Study-Abroad Grants

Awarding need-based stipends to members undertaking intensive language immersion programs in Spanish-speaking nations.

🥟

Free Weekly Refreshments

Serving hot café, fresh empanadas, and pan dulce at over 24 weekly conversation tables throughout the school year.

✈️

Member Travel Grants

Subsidizing flight, lodging, and registration fees for Yellow Jackets attending national Latinx leadership conferences.

How to Get Involved

All Georgia Tech undergraduate, graduate, alumni, faculty, and staff are warmly welcome. No prior fluency test or background required!

1

Join on GT Engage

Log in to the official Georgia Tech Engage portal with your GT credentials and request membership to receive official voting status and announcements.

2

Enter our GroupMe

Stay updated on informal dining meetups, impromptu study sessions in the CULC, carpooling to festivals, and flash reminders.

3

Come to a Conversation Table

Drop by the Student Center any Wednesday or Thursday at 6:00 PM. Grab an empanada, pull up a chair, and say ¡Hola!
